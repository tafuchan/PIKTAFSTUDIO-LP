2. What we collect

DotGacha (the "App") needs no account and can be played anonymously. We never ask for your phone number or postal address. You may optionally sign in with Google or Apple to carry your progress to a new device. Only if you sign in do we receive the display name and email address of that account.

Information about you

  • An anonymous identifier issued automatically by Firebase Anonymous Authentication
  • Display name and email address — only if you sign in with Google or Apple. Used to identify your account and carry your data to a new device.
  • The profile name and icon shown on the online rankings, if you use them
  • Your display board layouts, including any text you place on a board — saved to the cloud, and shown to others only if you choose to publish it
  • Game data: the pixel art you have collected, your coin and ticket balances, mission and login progress, and which decorations you own
  • Purchase information, if you buy anything in the App (the product bought, the transaction ID)
  • The content of any message you send us

Information collected automatically

  • An advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS), used to serve ads
  • Device information such as operating system version and device model
  • IP address and access timestamps

3. Why we use it

  • To run the App and to save and sync your progress

  • To answer your enquiries
  • To improve the App and produce aggregate statistics
  • To comply with the law

We take reasonable measures to prevent loss, damage or unauthorised disclosure of the information we hold. 12. If you live in the EU or the UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

The App is distributed in the European Union and the United Kingdom. If you live there, the following applies to you.

Controller

PIKTAF STUDIO is the controller of your personal data. Contact: piktafstudio@gmail.com

Our legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — running the game, saving and syncing your progress, processing purchases
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalised advertising and measuring its performance. We ask when you first open the App, and you can withdraw at any time
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — detecting and preventing abuse, and improving the App. We balance these against your rights and freedoms
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — keeping transaction records

Your rights

  • Access — a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correction of anything inaccurate
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — deletion of your data. You can also do this yourself: choose "Delete account" from the App's menu
  • Restriction of processing
  • Portability — your data in a structured, machine-readable format, and transmission to another controller where technically feasible
  • Objection — to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing
  • Withdrawal of consent — this does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL; elsewhere in the EU, your national authority; in the UK, the ICO

Write to piktafstudio@gmail.com to exercise any of these. We aim to respond within one month.

Transfers outside the EEA

The services we use — Google (Firebase, AdMob), RevenueCat and Cloudflare — may hold data on servers outside the EEA, mainly in the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework to keep your data protected.

Automated decision-making

We do not make automated decisions, or carry out profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you. Gacha draws are random and are not based on your personal data.
