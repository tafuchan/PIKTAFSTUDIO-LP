3. Analytics (Google Analytics)

To improve the Site, we use Google Analytics 4, an analytics service provided by Google LLC. It uses cookies to collect information such as:

  • URL and title of the pages you view, date and time, and time spent
  • Referrer (which site or search engine you came from)
  • Browser type, operating system, screen size and language settings
  • Approximate location (country and city level) derived from your IP address
  • Clicks on links to the app stores

This information is processed on an aggregated basis. We do not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address. Your IP address is used during processing by Google but is not stored in Google Analytics 4.

We do not use this data for advertising. Advertising-related cookies are disabled at all times by the Site's configuration.

4. Cookies and consent

  • Visitors from the EEA and the UK: Google Analytics is not loaded and no cookies are set until you press the accept button.
  • Visitors from other regions: Google Analytics is enabled by default. You can disable it at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page.
  • Theme preference (dark/light) and your consent choice: stored in your browser's localStorage. They are used only to display the Site and are never transmitted elsewhere.

If you withdraw consent, we stop loading Google Analytics and delete the Google Analytics cookies already stored (those beginning with _ga).

5. How to change or withdraw consent

  • Use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page at any time.
  • Delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Data retention

Event data collected through Google Analytics is retained according to the settings of our Google Analytics property (up to 14 months). Individual records are deleted automatically once that period expires.
